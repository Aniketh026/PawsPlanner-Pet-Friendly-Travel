# PawsPlanner-Pet-Friendly-Travel
A pet-friendly travel planner to discover nearby pet parks, veterinary clinics, pet shops, groomers, and hotels with interactive maps, weather forecasts, and itinerary planning.
# Paws Planner

## Overview

Welcome to Paws Planner, your go-to application for planning pet-friendly adventures around the world! Whether you're a pet owner or a pet lover, Paws Planner helps you discover and plan exciting activities for you and your furry friends in any location.

## Features

### 1. Location Search
On the landing page, users can easily search for any location worldwide. Simply enter the city or address you're interested in, and Paws Planner will provide you with a detailed map of the area.

### 2. Use My Location (GPS)
Don't know where to start? Click the GPS button next to the search bar to automatically detect your current location and find nearby pet-friendly spots instantly.

### 3. Search History
Your recent searches are saved locally and displayed as quick-access chips below the search bar. Click any chip to instantly re-run that search.

### 4. Pet-Friendly Points of Interest
Once you've selected a location, Paws Planner will display a map featuring various pet-friendly points of interest. These include parks, pet shops, veterinary services, groomers, dog parks, pet hotels, and more.

### 5. Category Filters
Refine your search with one-click category filters:

- All Pet — Show every pet-related place
- Dog Parks — Off-leash areas and dog parks
- Vets — Veterinary clinics and hospitals
- Pet Shops — Stores for pet supplies
- Groomers — Pet grooming services
- Pet Hotels — Pet-friendly accommodations

### 6. Detailed Information
Clicking on any point of interest will redirect you to links with detailed information about the place you're wanting to visit. You'll find important details such as address, contact information, opening hours, phone number, star rating, and any specific pet policies they may have.

### 7. Save Your Favorites
Found a place you love? Click the heart icon on any card to save it to your favorites. Access all saved places anytime via the Saved button in the navbar. Saved places persist across sessions using local storage.

### 8. Day Planner / Itinerary Builder
Plan your perfect pet-friendly day! Click the calendar icon on any place card to add it to your personal itinerary. The My Plan panel shows:

- All selected places in order
- Estimated total walking distance between stops
- The ability to remove places or clear the entire plan
- Share your plan via the Web Share API or copy to clipboard

### 9. Current Weather & 5-Day Forecast
Paws Planner shows real-time weather conditions for your searched location, including temperature, wind speed, and humidity. Plus, view a 5-day forecast to help you plan the perfect day out with your pet.

### 10. Dark / Light Theme
Toggle between light and dark modes using the moon/sun button in the navbar. Your preference is saved and persists across sessions.

### 11. Interactive Map with Pins
Every search populates the interactive Leaflet map with pins for each pet-friendly location. Click a pin to see the place name, and click the map card buttons to jump directly to that spot.

## Getting Started

- Search for a Location: Enter any desired location on the landing page search bar, or click the GPS button to use your current location.
- Filter by Category: Use the category buttons to narrow down results.
- Explore Points of Interest: Click on the cards below the map to discover pet-friendly places in the selected area.
- Save Favorites: Click the heart icon on any place card to save it for later.
- Build Your Plan: Click the calendar icon to add places to your day itinerary.
- View Details: Click on a point of interest which will redirect you to detailed information, user reviews, and any specific pet policies.
- Plan Your Day: Open "My Plan" to see your selected route, estimated walking distance, and share your itinerary!

## How to Run

Clone or download this repository to your local machine.

Open the `index.html` file directly in your browser, or start a local HTTP server (e.g., `npx serve` or VS Code Live Server) for the best experience.

API keys for Geoapify Places and OpenWeatherMap are already included in `assets/js/logic.js`, so the app works out of the box.
